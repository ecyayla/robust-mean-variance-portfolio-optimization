
The folder contains the daily prices data adjusted for dividends for:

- Dow Jones from 16/02/1990 to 07/04/2016;
- Euro Stoxx 50 from 22/05/2001 to 11/04/2016;
- FTSE 100 from 11/07/2002 to 11/04/2016;
- Hang Seng from 25/11/2005 to 11/04/2016;
- NASDAQ-100 from 03/11/2004 to 11/04/2016.

These prices are available both in .xlsx and in .mat extensions.

The sheet 'Assets' contains a matrix where each column is the daily prices of each asset.

The sheet 'Index' contains a column vector which is the daily prices of the market index.
